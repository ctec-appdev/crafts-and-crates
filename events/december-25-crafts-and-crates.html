December ’24 Crafts & Crates – Nicolay

December 21, 2024

Featured DJ’s

NICOLAY​

Nicolay (Matthijs Rook) has been a significant factor in the progression of hip-hop, R&B, and electronic music since the early 2000’s.

MAURICE DANIELS​

Maurice Daniels is an Atlanta-based beatmaker, DJ, and cohost of the “Beats Brews & Life” podcast where he mixes hip-hop culture with craft beer conversations.

EMPRESS RAH

Empress Rah is an Atlanta-based Open Format DJ, creative, and entrepreneur, bringing a diverse energy that undoubtedly unites people under one sound.

STLNDRMS

STLNDRMS (pronounced Stolen Drums) has built a career by sharing his knowledge with others by Live streaming his creative process.

dj mafioso

DJ Mafioso of Binkis Recs is an Atlanta turntablist and gear tech known for blending Latin and hip-hop sounds.
