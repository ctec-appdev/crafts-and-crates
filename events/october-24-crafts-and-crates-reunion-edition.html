October ’24 Crafts & Crates – Reunion Edition

October 19, 2024

Featured DJ’s

dj dainja

From NYC to ATL and beyond, DJ Dainja is always bringing the freshness on the tables and on his Beatminerz Radio show.

dj mafioso

DJ Mafioso of Binkis Recs is an Atlanta turntablist and gear tech known for blending Latin and hip-hop sounds. He’s also the trusted “DJ’s Doctor,” repairing and maintaining equipment for DJs across the city.

DJ SKy

DJ SKY has been translating his musical interests and moods through the turntables since ’93 who is always reppin’ of the Atlanta hiphop/DJ scene.

Performances By

BINKIS RECS!

Binkis Recs is an influential underground hip-hop group from Atlanta known for sharp lyrics and shaping the city’s indie rap scene.

I SELF DIVINE

As member of Micranots + other groups, In 2012, City Pages described I Self as “one of the most influential voices in Minnesota hip hop.

AOS

Master Student, Alchemist, Philosopher, HipHop junkie, Emcee, West Baltimore offspring. BINKIS representative. AOS is the real deal!

FORT KNOX

Fort Knox is an Atlanta DJ and host known for high-energy events and supporting the city’s hip-hop and DJ culture.

DILLON

Dillon has been in the Atlanta indy Hiphop scene since 2005. He’s a rapper, beatmaker, DJ, engineer & chef.

Unxnoun Ceeds

Unxoun Ceeds Atlanta based hip-hop duo known for their live stage presence and articulate rhymes.

CHIEF JUSTICE

Chief Justice hails from the ATL but ultimately took root in the fertile ground of Brooklyn, NY, where he currently holds court.

eddie meeks

Eddie Meeks is an Atlanta-based MC and producer known for boom-bap hip-hop and is one-third of the group Prophetix.
