FEATURED DJ’S

EMPRESS RAH

Empress Rah is an Atlanta-based Open Format DJ, creative, and entrepreneur, bringing a diverse energy that undoubtedly unites people under one sound.

EBONI ATL

Eboni ATL is an Atlanta-based DJ and Twitch host known for soulful, high-energy sets blending R&B, hip-hop, and funk.

DJ GHOST

DJ Ghost is an Atlanta-based DJ known as the “Mistress of the Blend,” a 5 Star Generals member, radio host, and mentor who blends music & education.

DJ AQUARIA

DJ Aquaria is an Atlanta-based turntablist known for genre-blending sets and mentoring new DJs. She performs locally and in global livestream events.
