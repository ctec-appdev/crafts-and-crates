September ’23 Crafts & Crates

September 16, 2023

Featured DJ’s

Jason Staten

Jason Staten came up in Michigan’s music scene, working with Juan Atkins and promoting Wu-Tang. He then moved to ATL and became a mixer at Hot 97.5.

SIR DANIEL

DJ Sir Daniel is an Atlanta-based vinyl DJ and historian specializing in celebrating Black music, especially women, through deeply curated throwback sets.

DJ SYNTHESIS

A technical wizard behind the boards, DJ Synthesis is one third of Psyche Origami and known for his eclectic hip-hop sets.

dj dainja

From NYC to ATL and beyond, DJ Dainja is always bringing the freshness on the tables and on his Beatminerz Radio show.

PEPPERMINT GATTY

DJ Peppermint Gatty is a vinyl DJ known for soulful sets blending classic R&B and Hip-Hop from the ’70s to early 2000s. She brings nostalgic vibes and community energy to every event.

dj mafioso

DJ Mafioso of Binkis Recs is an Atlanta turntablist and gear tech known for blending Latin and hip-hop sounds. He’s also the trusted “DJ’s Doctor,” repairing and maintaining equipment for DJs across the city.
