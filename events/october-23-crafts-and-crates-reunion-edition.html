October ’23 Crafts & Crates – Reunion Edition

October 21, 2023

Featured DJ’s

DJ SKy

DJ SKY has been translating his musical interests and moods through the turntables since ’93 who is always reppin’ of the Atlanta hiphop/DJ scene.

dj dainja

From NYC to ATL and beyond, DJ Dainja is always bringing the freshness on the tables and on his Beatminerz Radio show.

PEPPERMINT GATTY

DJ Peppermint Gatty is a vinyl DJ known for soulful sets blending classic R&B and Hip-Hop from the ’70s to early 2000s. She brings nostalgic vibes and community energy to every event.

dj mafioso

DJ Mafioso of Binkis Recs is an Atlanta turntablist and gear tech known for blending Latin and hip-hop sounds. He’s also the trusted “DJ’s Doctor,” repairing and maintaining equipment for DJs across the city.

Performances By

liun’s den

The All Nice & Decent Crew blends New York hip-hop and reggae, delivering a unique sound with emcees Ekundayo, Luz Wed, Terrance Arthur, & El Sun.

scienz of life

Scienz of Life is an ATL based hip-hop group known for jazz-influenced boom-bap and conscious lyrics. They’ve released underground staples since the late ’90s, including collaborations with MF DOOM.

Dug Boogie

Atlanta-based DJ and producer whose multi-genre takeovers—including classic hip-hop, soul, and funk—have energized venues like 529 and MJQ Concourse.

4-ize

4-IZE is an Atlanta MC known for sharp bars, animated style, and collaborations with Ludacris. He’s a staple in ATL’s hip-hop scene.

Prophetix

Prophetix is an Atlanta hip-hop duo known for gritty boom-bap and raw lyricism, active since the late ’90s.

AOS

Master Student, Alchemist, Philosopher, HipHop junkie, Emcee, West Baltimore offspring. BINKIS representative. AOS is the real deal!

DILLON

Dillon has been in the Atlanta indy Hiphop scene since 2005. He’s a rapper, beatmaker, DJ, engineer & chef.
