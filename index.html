Music, Food, Drinks
and Good Vibes

Crafts & Crates brings live hip-hop events to Halfway Crooks in Atlanta's historic Summerhill neighborhood. Pull up to the shipping containers at the Biergarten for great craft beer, amazing cocktails, awesome food, curated vinyl for sale, and top-tier DJs spinning the livest sets in the city.

Halfway Crooks Is the Spot!

Experience live sets from influential hip-hop artists and DJs as you sip on top-notch craft beer from world renowned Halfway Crooks Brewery. Crafts & Crates is a fun time for all ages and best of all it's no cover to come and enjoy the vibes. There's no better place to hang out and soak in real hip-hop culture. Salute!

  • Where

    Halfway Crooks Biergarten
    640 Reed St. SE 30312

  • When

    Every Third Saturday
